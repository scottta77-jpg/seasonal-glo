Everything You Need to Know.

Pause, breathe, and choose yourself again at our Harborne spa sanctuary.

What treatments are available?

We offer medical-grade Clinicare facials, HydraFacials, deep tissue and hot stone massages, intimate hot waxing, flawless makeup, and luxury eyelashes.

What happens on my first visit?

Your visit at our Harborne sanctuary begins with a friendly consultation to understand your skin or muscle goals, followed by a tailored treatment and expert aftercare.

How should I prepare?

Arrive a few minutes early at 10 Hampton Court Road, Harborne. For Ingrown Hair Vagacials, please book 48 hours before or after your waxing appointment.

How can I book or reschedule?

Book instantly online via our Fresha booking portal or contact us directly. For Couples Massage packages, join our waitlist to secure your future date.
